The rules Claude follows

House rules

  • Never change an author’s words. Add around them.
  • Say “article”, not “essay”.
  • Every number shows a public source, or says it is the author’s experience.
  • Toolkits: small tools, plain words (grade 6), one page or one sheet each.
  • Illustrations: objects and metaphors, never people.
  • Kyun hosts. He asks and connects; he never speaks for the author. He stays out of the story films.
  • English first. Devanagari only as a small second line.
