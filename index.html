III · Mimansa Shastrartha शास्त्रार्थ

We debate the HR questions nobody asks.

The questions come from the community. In each Shastrartha, one side defends how things are done today, the other argues for another way, and judges from HR, academics and business call the result.

It ends one of three ways

We settle it, we settle part of it, or we send it to research.

When we can’t settle it

The question goes to the Mimansa Research Trust. Sponsors pay for the research, but they don’t choose the question, the method or the findings. We publish every result.
