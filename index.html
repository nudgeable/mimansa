III · Why Mimansa

An old Indian way of asking hard questions.

Mimansa (मीमांसा) means deep inquiry. Its rule: don’t decide until you have named your doubt and heard the other side at its strongest.

Kyun (क्यों, “why”) is our host. He opens each article with its question. He never accuses. He asks, and waits.

How Kyun asks

One complaint we all hear: “Our managers don’t use what they learnt in leadership training.”

  1. Step 1

    Name the doubt

    संशय

    “How do we know they aren’t? What would using it even look like?”

  2. Step 2

    Hear the other side

    पूर्वपक्ष

    “Same managers, same meetings, same scores six months on. It looks like nothing changed.”

  3. Step 3

    Answer, and conclude

    निर्णय

    “We set no expectations and no measures. So we have no evidence either way.”

The conclusion

We can’t say they aren’t applying it. Before the next programme, agree two behaviours and a date to check.
