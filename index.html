Why Mimansa

What’s the one HR question you’ve never asked out loud?

Untold stories

Real experiences from Indian workplaces come up in corridors and private chats, and stop there. Names changed, shared with consent, they deserve to be heard.

Unasked questions

Exit interviews, PIPs, bell curves. We run them every year and rarely ask if they work.

Mimansa exists to make sharing real stories and asking tough questions normal in Indian HR. It is a platform and a community, built by the whole HR fraternity, not a few voices. Indian context, Indian data, Indian workplaces.

The way: shastrartha. India’s old tradition of debate as a path to truth. Kyun (क्यों, “why”) hosts it. He asks, and waits.
