How we run the site, without becoming tech people.

Three questions: where the site lives, how readers sign up for the letter, and how a piece goes from our heads to a page. Short answers first.

Kyun

Pick the tool that does the boring parts for you.

The short answer

Run Mimansa on Ghost(Pro), a hosted publishing platform with sign-ups and newsletters built in. Claude builds one custom Mimansa theme from these pages. Not pure HTML, not a custom Astro build, and no Medusa.

Sign-ups

Built in

Readers join with their email and get a one-tap sign-in link. No passwords to manage, and no payment gateway needed.

Everything free

Nothing locked

Every article, film and tool is open to everyone. Readers join only for the fortnightly letter and to follow what they send us.

The fortnightly letter

Built in

Every member can get the letter by email. No Mailchimp, no list exports. We press Publish and choose “send as newsletter”.

Every fortnight

Who does what

Authors (any of us)

Write the article or collect the story. Send it through the Studio form, or drop the Word file in the shared Drive folder.

Claude

Builds the page in the Mimansa design, adds Kyun, notes, sources and the toolkit, checks every rule, and posts it to Ghost as a draft.

One editor on duty

Reads the draft in Ghost, asks for changes in chat, presses Publish, and sends it as the letter. About one hour.

The Studio page in this draft shows the forms and the inbox. On Ghost, the same forms run on Tally, and the inbox is our email plus a sheet.

Later, if we charge

Stripe is the catch

Ghost’s paid tiers only work through Stripe, and Stripe is invite-only for Indian businesses: new Indian companies cannot sign up on their website. Everything on the site is free, so none of this matters until we decide to charge for something.

When we decide to charge, we pick one of three routes:

  1. Charge for advisory and research, not for reading. Keep the site free, and sell reports, workshops and advisory through Razorpay payment pages. No change to the site. Our pick, and it fits a Gartner-style business.
  2. Ask Stripe for an India invite now. Free to request, no promise. If it comes through, paid tiers in Ghost are a switch.
  3. Move to a platform that takes Razorpay for paid reading. Members export as one file, but the design would be rebuilt.
