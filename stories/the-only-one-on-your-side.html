“He says everyone is against me. That he’s the only one on my side.”

Trailer · 40 s
What happened

Resigned. Withdrawn. Resigned.

It was 2020, the lockdown, and everyone worked from home. In three days, the HR head, Nandini, got three notifications about one employee, Ruhi. She resigned. She withdrew it. She resigned again. Then she stopped answering calls and email.

Her brother called from another city: the family could not reach her either. When Ruhi finally picked up, she said she wanted to leave because of her manager. A complaint followed, and an internal committee looked into it. What it could prove, and what HR did next, is the rest of the film.

Said · and meant

Three things that were said, and what they meant.

“I’m the only one on your side.”It sounds supportive, but it cuts her off from everyone else.
“Probably just a bad day, ma’am.”Saying it once is fair. Saying it twice should make you check.
“Nothing was proven.”Not proven is not the same as no concerns.
Chapters

Five chapters, one point of view.

We follow Nandini the whole way. You only know what she knew, when she knew it.

  1. Three notificationsResigned, withdrawn, resigned. Then silence.
  2. The letterA call from her brother, and a letter she wrote home.
  3. The only one on your sideRuhi names her manager. A committee is set up.
  4. What can be provedChats, a receipt, and a finding: not substantiated.
  5. What HR can’t sayA promotion is proposed. HR says no, and can’t say why.
For HR professionals

Three lessons from the case.

  1. A missing employee is a welfare question before it is an HR process.Act on silence early, and through every channel.
  2. “I’m the only one on your side” is a boundary problem, whatever the intent.Managers can support people without becoming their only support.
  3. “Not proven” is not the same as “no concerns.”A closed inquiry can still count when you judge someone’s readiness to lead.
The question it leaves

Your decision is visible. Your reasons are confidential. How do you protect trust in HR when you can’t explain yourself?
